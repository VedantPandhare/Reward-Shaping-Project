What am I seeing?

Both landers fly the same terrain with the same physics. They differ only in the reward they were trained on.

Hover hack: trained with +1 per frame while upright and slow. Landing ends the episode and stops the payments, so the agent learned to hover until the 1000-frame time limit — its training reward goes up while the behaviour gets worse.

Delta fix: pays for improvement (potential-based shaping) plus a small time cost. Hovering earns nothing, so landing wins.

The chart under each lander splits every frame's reward into its parts. The magenta bonus bar dominating it is the tell. The “custom-bonus share” meter is the same number the project's hacking detector uses.

Physics is a port of Gymnasium's LunarLander-v3 (Box2D via planck.js); the policies are the real trained PPO networks, exported as JSON.
