Glossary

Reward
The points an agent receives each frame. It is never told what to do; it learns whatever earns the most points.
Game score vs training reward
Game score is the real game's reward and is the fair measure of how well it flew. Training reward adds the custom bonus the agent was trained on. When the two diverge, the agent is chasing the bonus.
Reward hacking
The agent maximises the reward you wrote, not the goal you meant. Here the goal is "land softly on the pad".
Hover-hack agent
Trained with +1 point per frame while upright and slow. Landing ends the game and stops those points, so it learned to hover until the 1000-frame limit instead of landing.
Improvement reward ("delta")
The fix. It pays only for improvement since the last frame (potential-based shaping). Over a whole episode these payments cancel out, so hovering earns nothing and landing wins.
Time cost
A small penalty every frame, so wasting time costs points. We tested whether it was needed for the fix; it was not.
Bonus share meter
How much of the agent's total reward comes from the custom bonus. The project's hacking detector raises an alert when it passes 50% together with a high timeout rate.
Timed out
The run reached 1000 frames without ending: hovering, not landing.

Physics is a port of Gymnasium's LunarLander-v3 (Box2D via planck.js). The policies are the real trained PPO networks exported as JSON.
